🔥 Trending & popular events

Scored weekly (Sundays) from what people actually click, add to calendar, and share — see the digest job spec for the exact thresholds. 🔥 trending = running well above that event's own usual pace; ⭐ popular = simply the highest raw volume this week, regardless of trend.

🎯 Referrals driven

How many outbound source-clicks you're actually sending to trending & popular events, and whether that number is growing week over week. Pulled from the same weekly scoring as the card above, so the events shown always match what's currently badged 🔥 or ⭐.

🔎 Search insights

What people actually type into the search box, last 30 days. Zero-result searches are the useful signal — someone looked for something specific and Playroute had nothing to show them, a direct pointer to what to add next.

A/B tests

Experiments

🧪 Recommended for you — A/B test

Half of browsers get the ✨ Recommended strip (treatment), half never see it (control). A visitor keeps their group on later visits; each visit counts as one session. Treatment visitors only actually see the strip once they've clicked 3+ events, so "saw strip" shows how many were really exposed. The significance-tested rows count clicks on any event card across the whole site, not just the strip — that's the real question (does the strip change overall behavior). Strip-only clicks and 👍/👎 votes are listed separately.
